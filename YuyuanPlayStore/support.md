A collection of local puzzles, drawing tools and audio playback for iPhone and iPad. Version 1.0.0 is free, with no account, advertising or active purchases.

## Common questions / 常见问题

### How do I play Calc N?

Choose Calc 12, Calc 24, Calc 36 or Practice from the mode selector. Select a number, an operation, then another number. Combine all numbers to reach the target. Use Reset to retry or Show answer for a hint.

选择目标数字或练习模式，按“数字 → 运算符 → 另一个数字”的顺序操作，将所有数字组合成目标值。可重置或查看答案。

### How do I play my own audio?

Open Music Player from the home menu and select an audio file using Files. The app keeps a local copy and your recent tracks. Use Play/Pause, the position slider and 1x/2x/3x repeat controls. These repeat settings select the number of plays, not playback speed. Playback also supports system and lock-screen controls.

从首页打开音乐播放器，通过“文件”选取音频。应用保留本地副本及最近播放记录。1x/2x/3x 表示播放次数，并非播放速度。

### Why can't I save a drawing?

When you save an image, allow add-only Photos access. You can manage the permission in iOS Settings. Drawing and other features work without this permission.

保存作品时请允许向相册添加图片；可以在系统设置中管理该权限。绘图及其他功能不依赖此权限。

### Where is my data stored?

Progress, preferences and imported audio copies stay on your device. Deleting the app removes its local data, subject to Apple's backup and restore behavior. Original audio files and images already saved to Photos are retained in their original locations.

进度、设置及导入的音频副本保存在设备上。删除应用会移除本地应用数据，具体受系统备份与恢复机制影响。原始音频及已导出到相册的图片保留在原位置。
