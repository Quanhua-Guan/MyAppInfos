# YuYuanPlayStore Privacy Policy

Effective date and last updated: October 14, 2026

This policy applies to YuYuanPlayStore for iPhone and iPad, version 1.0.0. The app does not collect or upload personal data. This release has no accounts, advertising, analytics, tracking or active in-app purchases.

## Local data

Game progress, best scores, preferences and saved challenge state are processed and stored locally on your device. We do not receive this information.

## Audio files

When you select audio using Apple's Files picker, the app stores a local copy, file bookmarks, recent-track metadata and playback position so you can play and reopen the track. Audio and its metadata are not uploaded to us. Your selected file provider, including a cloud storage provider, may process the original file under its own privacy policy.

## Photos permission

The app requests add-only Photos permission when you choose to save an image. It does not read your photo library. Saved images are managed by Photos and may be included in your system photo backup if you enable it.

## Retention and deletion

Local progress and settings remain on your device until they are reset or the app is deleted. Deleting the app removes its local data and imported audio copies from that device, subject to Apple's backup and restore behavior. Images already saved to Photos and original audio files remain in their original locations. We hold no server-side app data to delete.

## Third parties and system services

The app includes AlertToast for local interface messages; it does not include advertising or analytics SDKs. Apple may process App Store, diagnostic and backup information according to your device settings and [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). Such processing is governed by Apple's policies.

## Children

The same local-data practices apply to all users. We do not collect personal information from children, and this version offers no purchases or advertisements.

## Your choices

You can manage Photos permission and backups in iOS Settings, remove exported images in Photos, and remove local app data by deleting the app. No account is required, so there is no account-deletion process.

## Policy updates

We will update this page and its date if the app's data practices change.

## Contact

For support or privacy questions, contact the developer at [xinmuheart@163.com](mailto:xinmuheart@163.com). Please avoid including audio files, photographs or other personal information unless needed to explain your request.
